LAUNCH BONUS PROGRAMME TERMS

One-time ₹10,000 launch bonus for Dudes

Version 1.0 · Effective from: 10 November 2026

These terms (the "Bonus Terms") are issued by Duedoh TravelTech India Private Limited ("Duedoh") and form part of the Dude Partner Agreement.

1.  THE BONUS IN ONE LINE

Complete 20 qualifying Dude Lite or Dude Pro bookings within one 3-month Bonus Cycle and earn a one-time ₹10,000 launch bonus.


2.  BONUS CYCLES

2.1 The programme runs in consecutive cycles of 3 calendar months (each a "Bonus Cycle"). The first Bonus Cycle runs from 1 February 2027 to 30 April 2027; the next from 1 May 2027 to 31 July 2027; and so on, until Duedoh ends the programme under Clause 8.

2.2 Bookings completed before 1 February 2027 are paid at normal earnings but do not count towards the bonus.

3.  WHO CAN TAKE PART

3.1 Any Dude who is approved and active on the Platform, with a 100% complete profile and PAN or Aadhaar on record, may take part in any Bonus Cycle.

4.  QUALIFYING BOOKINGS

4.1 A qualifying booking is a Dude Lite or Dude Pro booking that is: (a) booked and paid by a Tripper through the Platform; (b) completed by you; and (c) not cancelled, refunded or under unresolved dispute.

4.2 For a multi-day trip, each completed day counts as one qualifying booking.

4.3 Dude Ask sessions do not qualify.

4.4 Bookings made by you, your relatives or associates, or any booking arranged to earn the bonus rather than for a genuine Tripper need, do not qualify.

5.  EARNING THE BONUS

5.1 If you complete 20 qualifying bookings within the same Bonus Cycle, you earn the launch bonus of ₹10,000 (Rupees Ten Thousand only).

5.2 Reset. If you do not reach 20 qualifying bookings by the end of a Bonus Cycle, no bonus is earned for that cycle, and your count restarts from zero in the next Bonus Cycle. Bookings do not carry over between cycles.

5.3 One time only. Each Dude can earn the launch bonus only once. After you have earned it, the programme no longer applies to you.

5.4 Your progress in the current Bonus Cycle is shown in the app.

6.  PAYMENT

6.1 The bonus is paid once, in your next 15-day withdrawal after you complete your 20th qualifying booking, after deduction of tax at source as applicable.

6.2 The bonus is a one-time incentive. It is not salary, wages or a guaranteed payment, and it is not paid for joining or registering.

7.  FRAUD AND MISCONDUCT

7.1 Duedoh may refuse or recover the bonus if it is earned through fake, self-arranged or related-party bookings, or any other fraud, or if you are removed from the Platform for misconduct.

8.  CHANGES TO THE PROGRAMME

8.1 Duedoh may change or end the programme, or any future Bonus Cycle, by giving at least 15 days' notice in the app. Any change will not affect a bonus you have already earned, except under Clause 7.

9.  GENERAL

9.1 Duedoh's decision on whether a booking qualifies shall be final, save for manifest error. Disputes may be raised with the Grievance Officer. These Bonus Terms are governed by the laws of India.
