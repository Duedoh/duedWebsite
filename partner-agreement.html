By tapping "I agree" in the Duedoh Dude app, or by using the Duedoh platform, you confirm that you have read, understood and accepted this Agreement, including the Code of Conduct in Schedule 1. Duedoh records the date, time and version of your acceptance.

1.  ABOUT DUEDOH AND YOUR ROLE

1.1 Duedoh operates a technology platform (the Duedoh apps and duedoh.com, together the "Platform") that connects travellers and visitors ("Trippers") with verified local partners ("Dudes") who provide on-ground assistance, local mobility and remote assistance.

1.2 Duedoh is an intermediary. Duedoh provides the Platform; you provide the services directly to the Tripper. Duedoh does not itself provide the on-ground services.

1.3 You are an independent partner, not an employee. Nothing in this Agreement creates employment, partnership, agency or joint venture between you and Duedoh. You are not entitled to salary, wages or employment benefits from Duedoh, and you are responsible for your own taxes, subject to tax deducted at source by Duedoh where required by law.

1.4 You choose your own availability. You are free to work with other platforms or businesses, including ride-hailing and delivery platforms, when you are not on a Duedoh booking.

2.  SERVICES YOU MAY OFFER

2.1 Dude Lite: accompanying a Tripper on foot or by public transport for a half day (5 hours) or full day (9 hours), helping with directions, local transport, food, shopping, language and other local needs.

2.2 Dude Pro: the same assistance, using your own bike, auto-rickshaw or car to move the Tripper around, for a half day (5 hours) or full day (9 hours), within the free-distance band shown in the app.

2.3 Dude Ask: remote, per-minute assistance to Trippers by in-app call, chat or guidance, from wherever you are.

2.4 Multi-day trips: where a Tripper books a multi-day trip, you shall remain available to the Tripper for the duration of the trip as described in the booking.

2.5 The services, durations, free distance and other details of each service are as shown in the app at the time of booking, and may be updated by Duedoh from time to time.

3.  ELIGIBILITY AND VERIFICATION

3.1 To be a Dude you must: (a) be at least 18 years old; (b) know your city well; (c) speak Telugu (or the local language of your city) and basic English or Hindi; (d) provide a valid government-issued photo ID; and (e) provide your PAN or Aadhaar (required for tax purposes).

3.2 For Dude Pro, you must also provide and keep valid at all times: your driving licence, vehicle registration certificate (RC), vehicle insurance, pollution-under-control certificate, and any registration, permit or licence required by law for carrying passengers for reward in the vehicle you use. You are solely responsible for your vehicle's legal compliance.

3.3 Duedoh's authorised team manually reviews and verifies the identity and documents you submit. Verification confirms your identity and documents; it is not a guarantee of your conduct, and you remain fully responsible for your conduct during every booking.

3.4 You confirm that all information and documents you provide are true, current and your own. Duedoh may suspend Pro bookings immediately if any Pro document expires or is found invalid, while you may continue to accept Lite and Dude Ask bookings if otherwise eligible.

3.5 You shall complete Duedoh's onboarding training and keep your profile 100% complete and up to date.

4.  BOOKINGS

4.1 Trippers choose and book a Dude through the Platform. You will be notified of each booking request in the app.

4.2 Reject window. You may reject a booking within 10 minutes of receiving the request. If you do not reject it within 10 minutes, the booking is treated as accepted.

4.3 Cancelling after acceptance. After the reject window, you may cancel only by emailing dude.support@duedoh.com. A late cancellation lowers your Dude Score and may lead to suspension of new bookings for 15 to 27 days. No money is deducted from your earnings for a late cancellation.

4.4 Contact with the Tripper. Calls and chats with Trippers take place through the Platform's masked calling and in-app chat. Contact opens 10 minutes before the booking start time.

4.5 Seat limits. For Dude Pro, you shall not carry more passengers than the seat limit of your vehicle category as shown in the app (Bike: 1 passenger).

4.6 Overtime and distance. Overtime is chargeable only if the Tripper approves it in the app before it begins. Distance beyond the free-distance band is charged to the Tripper at the rate shown in the app.

4.7 Tripper no-show. If the Tripper does not arrive within 30 minutes of the booking start time and cannot be reached through the app, you may mark the booking as a no-show in the app.

4.8 Ending a trip when unsafe. If at any time you feel unsafe, you may end the trip. Contact Duedoh support immediately through the in-app emergency option (where available) or the support numbers. You will still be paid for the booking, subject to Duedoh's review.

5.  EARNINGS AND PAYMENTS

5.1 All payments for bookings are collected from Trippers through the Platform by Duedoh's licensed payment partner. You shall not accept payment for any booking outside the Platform.

5.2 Your earnings for each booking are the listed price of the service less Duedoh's commission applicable to that service, plus: (a) 100% of any charges for distance beyond the free-distance band; (b) 100% of any approved overtime; and (c) 100% of any tips paid by the Tripper through the Platform. Duedoh's commission is never charged on distance, overtime or tips. The commission applicable to each service is shown in the app.

5.3 Any discount offered to a Tripper is borne by Duedoh and does not reduce your earnings.

5.4 Cancellation compensation. Where a Tripper cancels between 12 and 24 hours before the start, or cancels within 12 hours or does not show, you will receive the compensation shown for that case in Duedoh's Cancellation & Refund Policy.

5.5 Withdrawals. Your earnings are credited to your Duedoh wallet and may be withdrawn every 15 days to your bank account or UPI ID registered in the app.

5.6 Statement. Each payout shows your gross earnings, Duedoh's commission, tax deducted at source (shown as ₹0 where nil), distance, overtime, tips and the net amount paid.

5.7 Taxes. Duedoh will deduct tax at source as required by law. If your PAN or Aadhaar is not on record, a higher rate may apply. During the pilot, Duedoh bears any GST payable on its own commission and platform fee.

5.8 Duedoh may withhold or adjust earnings from a booking that is under dispute, found fraudulent, or was not actually delivered, pending review.

SCHEDULE 1

Dude Code of Conduct

Every Dude represents Duedoh in front of a traveller who is new to the city. These rules protect Trippers, protect you, and protect every other Dude.

ALWAYS

(a) Be on time. If you will be late, inform the Tripper through the app before the start time.

(b) Be polite, patient and respectful to every Tripper, whatever their background, gender, religion, language or origin.

(c) Keep your profile photo current and identify yourself clearly when you meet the Tripper.

(d) Follow traffic laws, wear a helmet and provide a helmet for any pillion passenger, and drive safely.

(e) Keep the Tripper's safety and comfort first, and help them reach fair prices and genuine services.

(f) Respond to Duedoh support immediately during a booking.

(g) Report any incident, accident or safety concern to Duedoh support immediately.

NEVER

(a) Consume alcohol or drugs before or during a booking.

(b) Make any sexual, romantic or inappropriate remark, gesture or advance towards a Tripper.

(c) Enter a Tripper's hotel room, or invite a Tripper to your home.

(d) Take a Tripper anywhere they have not agreed to go, or pressure them into any purchase, shop or service.

(e) Accept commissions from shops or vendors for steering Trippers to them without the Tripper's knowledge.

(f) Ask for or accept any payment outside the Platform, other than a tip the Tripper freely offers through the app.

(g) Carry more passengers than your vehicle category allows, or allow anyone else to deliver the booking in your place.

(h) Photograph, record or share any Tripper's photos, location or details without their consent.

(i) Describe Duedoh's services to anyone as guaranteed safe, or make promises on Duedoh's behalf.

Breach of this Code may lead to suspension or permanent removal under Clause 11 of the Agreement.
