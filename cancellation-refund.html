CANCELLATION & REFUND POLICY

How cancellations, no-shows and refunds work on Duedoh

Version 1.0 · Effective from: 10 November 2026

This policy is issued by Duedoh TravelTech India Private Limited ("Duedoh") and forms part of the Terms of Service for Trippers and the Dude Partner Agreement. Every amount is shown in the app before you pay.

1.1 Platform fee. The platform fee pays for work already done (taking the booking, matching a Dude and holding the slot) and is not refunded when you cancel, except as set out in Section 2.

1.2 Where the cancellation charge goes. The cancellation charge in the 12–24 hour window, and the 50% retained for cancellations under 12 hours or no-shows, are paid to your Dude as compensation for the blocked time.

1.3 No-show. If you do not arrive within 30 minutes of the booking start time and cannot be reached through the app, the booking is treated as a no-show.

1.4 Multi-day trips. Cancellation is calculated on the trip as a whole, based on the time remaining before the trip's start. Once a multi-day trip has started, days not yet begun may be cancelled under the same windows, counted from the start of each such day.

1.5 Dude Ask. A Dude Ask session cannot be cancelled once it has started. Unused minutes are not refunded.

2.  IF YOUR DUDE CANCELS OR DOES NOT ARRIVE

2.1 If your Dude cancels after accepting, Duedoh will offer you other available Dudes for the same time so that you can choose a replacement.

2.2 If you do not choose a replacement, no replacement Dude is available, or your Dude does not arrive, you will receive a full refund, including the platform fee.

2.3 If a Dude Ask session cannot be connected or is cut off by a failure on the Dude's or Duedoh's side, you will receive a refund for the session or the unused minutes.

3.  OTHER CASES

3.1 Trip ended for safety reasons. If a booking is ended early for safety reasons, Duedoh will review the case and decide any refund fairly based on what happened.

3.2 Service not as described. If the service delivered was materially different from what you booked, raise a complaint within 24 hours of the end of the booking. Duedoh will review it and may offer a full or partial refund.

3.3 Overtime, extra distance and tips that have been used or paid are not refundable, except where charged in error.

4.  HOW AND WHEN REFUNDS ARE PAID

4.1 Refunds are initiated within 24 hours of the cancellation or of Duedoh's decision on a complaint, to the original payment method.

4.2 Refunds usually reach your account within 5 to 7 working days, depending on your bank or payment provider.

4.3 Refund amounts are rounded in your favour.

5.  QUESTIONS AND COMPLAINTS
