PRIVACY POLICY

How Duedoh collects, uses and protects personal data

Version 1.0 · Effective from: 10 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Duedoh TravelTech India Private Limited (CIN: U79110AP2026PTC124781), a company incorporated under the Companies Act, 2013, with its registered office at 39-1-24/6/18A, Singarimetta, Ayyappanagar, Visakhapatnam – 530007, Andhra Pradesh ("Duedoh", "we") collects, uses, shares and protects personal data of Trippers, Dudes and other users of the Duedoh apps and duedoh.com (the "Platform"). Duedoh is the Data Fiduciary for this data under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the rules framed under it.

By tapping "I agree" in the Duedoh Tripper or Dude app, or by using the Duedoh platform, you confirm that you have read, understood and accepted this Privacy Policy and give the consents described in it. Duedoh records the date, time and version of your acceptance.

1.  PERSONAL DATA WE COLLECT

From

Data

All users

Name, mobile number, email, profile photo, city, account login details (including one-time passwords sent by email), device and app information, app usage and support conversations

Trippers

Booking details (dates, places, group size), payment details processed by our payment partner, ratings and complaints

Dudes

Government ID, PAN or Aadhaar, date of birth, address, languages, bank account or UPI ID, earnings and payout records, ratings, Dude Score; for Dude Pro: driving licence, vehicle registration, insurance, pollution and permit documents, vehicle photos

During active bookings

Live location of the Dude and the Tripper, trip start and end times, distance travelled, emergency alerts

Calls and chats

Masked-call records (time and duration) and in-app chat messages


1.1 We collect live location only during an active booking, and stop when the booking ends.

1.2 The app stores some data temporarily on your device (cache) so that it works faster and offline. You can clear this by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app.

2.  WHY WE USE IT

(a) To create and manage your account, and to verify the identity and documents of Dudes.

(b) To arrange, run and support bookings, including matching Trippers with Dudes and masked calling.

(c) For safety: live location during bookings, emergency support and incident investigation.

(d) To process payments, payouts, refunds, bonuses and taxes, including tax deduction at source.

(e) To handle ratings, complaints and disputes, and to prevent fraud and misuse.

(f) To improve the Platform, and to send service messages. Promotional messages are sent only with your consent, and you can opt out at any time.

(g) To comply with law and with lawful requests from authorities.

3.  CONSENT

3.1 We process your personal data on the basis of the consent you give when you accept this Policy, and where otherwise permitted by law. You may withdraw consent at any time by writing to grievance@duedoh.com or deleting your account. Withdrawal does not affect processing already done, and we may be unable to continue providing services that depend on that data.

4.  WHO WE SHARE IT WITH

4.1 Between Trippers and Dudes: only what is needed for a booking, such as name, photo, rating, vehicle details (for Dude Pro) and live location during the booking. Personal phone numbers are not shared; calls are masked.

4.2 Service providers that process data on our behalf, under contract and only for the purposes in this Policy: cloud hosting (Microsoft Azure); payment processing (Cashfree Payments); maps and location services; masked calling; and email delivery, including one-time passwords.

4.2A Identity and vehicle documents submitted by Dudes are reviewed manually by authorised Duedoh staff and are not shared with any outside verification service.

4.3 Authorities: where required by law, court order, or to protect someone's life or safety, including sharing location with police or emergency services in an emergency.

4.4 We do not sell your personal data.

5.  WHERE AND HOW WE STORE IT

5.1 Data is stored on Microsoft Azure cloud infrastructure. We use reasonable security safeguards, including encryption in transit, access controls limited to authorised staff, and audit logs.

5.2 If a personal data breach occurs, we will notify affected users and the Data Protection Board of India as required by law.

6.  HOW LONG WE KEEP IT

6.1 Booking, payment and tax records: 3 years from the date of the transaction, or longer if required by law.

6.2 Dude identity and vehicle documents: deleted within 90 days of the account being closed, unless required for an ongoing dispute, investigation or legal obligation.

6.3 Live location data: kept with the trip record for the same period as booking records, for safety and dispute purposes.

6.4 Other data is deleted or anonymised when it is no longer needed for the purposes in Section 2.

7.  YOUR RIGHTS

7.1 Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you have the right to: (a) access a summary of your personal data and how it is processed; (b) correct, complete or update it; (c) have it erased, subject to legal retention requirements; (d) withdraw consent; (e) have your grievance addressed; and (f) nominate another person to exercise your rights in the event of your death or incapacity.

7.2 To exercise any right, write to grievance@duedoh.com from your registered email or contact us through the app.

8.  CHILDREN

8.1 The Platform is for users aged 18 and above. We do not knowingly create accounts for, or collect personal data from, anyone under 18. If you believe we have, contact us and we will delete it.

9.  CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

9.1 We may update this Policy from time to time. Material changes will be notified in the app before they take effect.
